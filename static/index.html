Paste a link. Keep the video.

Works with YouTube videos and Shorts, and Instagram reels and posts. Pick a quality, and the file lands in your downloads folder.

Three steps, about ten seconds

  1. Copy the linkUse Share → Copy link in the YouTube or Instagram app, or copy it from your browser's address bar.
  2. Paste it aboveClipdrop reads the title, length and every quality the video is available in.
  3. Choose and savePick a resolution for MP4, or Audio for an MP3. The download starts when it's ready.

Questions

Why is 1080p and above slower?

Above 720p, video and audio are delivered as separate streams. Clipdrop downloads both and joins them into one MP4, which adds a few seconds at the end.

An Instagram link says it needs a logged-in session. What now?

Instagram hides many posts from logged-out visitors. Export your browser cookies to a file named cookies.txt, put it next to app.py, and restart the server.

Where do the files go?

Straight to your browser's downloads folder. The server keeps its temporary copy for 30 minutes, then deletes it.

Can I download a whole playlist?

Not yet. A playlist link downloads the single video it points to. For an Instagram carousel, you get the first video in the post.
